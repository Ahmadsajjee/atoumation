📖 How to Send Emails to Your Group

✅ Ready to Use! All email workflows are configured and ready.

🚀 Send Email to Group (Including Dania):

  1. Click the "Send Email to Group" button above
  2. Click "Run workflow" on GitHub
  3. Fill in the email list:
    • Default: dania.haj.qassem@gmail.com
    • Or add more: dania.haj.qassem@gmail.com, email2@gmail.com, email3@gmail.com
    • (Separate emails with commas)
  4. Fill in subject and message
  5. Click "Run workflow"
  6. Email will be sent to all recipients in seconds!

📝 All Available Workflows:

  • Send Email to Group - Multiple recipients ⭐ NEW
  • Send Test Email - Test to yourself
  • Send Custom Email - Single recipient
  • Create Folder - Create folders in repo
  • Hello World - Auto-run on push
💡 Tip: Emails are sent from your Gmail account to any email addresses you specify. You can add or remove recipients anytime!
