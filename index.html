📖 How to Use Email Workflows

✅ Status: Email credentials are configured and ready to use!

Quick Start - Send Test Email:

  1. Click the "Send Test Email Now" button above
  2. Click "Run workflow" button on GitHub
  3. Click "Run workflow" again to confirm
  4. Wait 5-10 seconds for the email to arrive in your Gmail inbox
  5. Check your Gmail - you should see an email from your account!

Send Custom Emails:

  1. Click the "Send Email" workflow button
  2. Click "Run workflow" on GitHub
  3. Fill in:
    • Recipient email: Where to send (any email address)
    • Email subject: What's the subject
    • Email body: What's the message
  4. Click "Run workflow"
  5. Email will be sent in seconds!
💡 Tip: Emails are sent from your Gmail account. Check spam folder if you don't see the test email.
